# Make it yours

1. Set the public business name, URL, description, email, and tagline in `config/site.ts`.
2. Replace the fictional services in `config/services.ts`.
3. Rewrite the home, about, and contact pages for your audience.
4. Set colors and sizing in `app/globals.css`; chart styling lives in `app/diagrams.css`.
5. Replace the neutral favicon in `public/favicon.svg` with your own mark.
6. Update the demo notice in `app/components/footer.tsx` only when the content represents your real business.
7. Replace the privacy and terms worksheets with appropriate policies.
8. Keep the public URL synchronized with `SITE_ORIGIN` in `wrangler.jsonc`.

The visual gallery lives at `/charts`. Its metrics and estimator assumptions are fictional and visibly labeled. Change both the data and the labels if adapting them to real evidence. Diagram content is separate from the reusable component in `app/components/system-diagram-data.ts`.

No external fonts, stock photos, tracking pixels, customer logos, or source business images are bundled. Retain the applicable Apache and MIT license notices when redistributing code.
